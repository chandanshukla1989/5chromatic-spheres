# Use of generative AI

Generative-AI tools were used extensively in this project: for computational exploration, for
implementing the search and verification scripts, and for drafting the manuscript and documentation.

The author, Chandan Kumar Shukla, is the sole author and is responsible for all claims. The author
chose the research direction from the options considered, set the acceptance and verification criteria
(exact algebraic coordinates, certificate-based and independently cross-checked proofs, no reliance on
floating-point distances — standards he adopted after his 2024 correspondence on an earlier attempt),
reviewed the results, decided which constructions and claims to include, and curated the final
artifacts.

Every claim of the paper is backed by a certificate or an exact computation that can be re-checked
without trusting the software that produced it: `./verify_all.sh` re-runs all checks (see
`REPRODUCIBILITY.md`).
