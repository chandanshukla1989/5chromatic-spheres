# Use of AI tools

The author, Chandan Kumar Shukla, initiated and led this project and is its sole author. He posed the
problem and its goal, provided the computing resources and AI tools, chose the research direction from the
options considered, and set the acceptance and verification criteria: exact algebraic (radical)
coordinates, certificate-based proofs, independent cross-checks, and no reliance on floating-point
distances — standards he adopted from his own earlier work on the Hadwiger–Nelson problem (since 2023).
He reviewed the results, had the artifacts reviewed independently, decided which constructions and
claims to include, and takes full responsibility for all claims.

AI tools were used for computational exploration, for implementing the search and verification scripts,
and for drafting the manuscript and documentation.

Every claim of the paper is backed by a certificate or an exact computation that can be re-checked
without trusting the software that produced it: `./verify_all.sh` re-runs all checks (see
`REPRODUCIBILITY.md`).
